PASTA 101
PERFECT PASTA in 30 seconds
STEP 1 Big pot. Rolling boil. Bring a big pot of water to a rolling boil.
STEP 2 Salt it generously. It should taste pleasantly salty.
STEP 3 Add pasta. Stir. Stir for the first minute so it doesn't stick.
STEP 4 One minute less. Cook 1 minute under the package time, so it keeps a little bite.
STEP 5 Save the pasta water. Before draining, save a mug of the starchy water.
STEP 6 Toss with sauce. Add a splash of that pasta water. Glossy, silky, done.
Buon appetito! Save this for dinner tonight
